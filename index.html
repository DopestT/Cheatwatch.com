About this display

This archive distinguishes between convictions, guilty pleas, court rulings, official findings, civil enforcement, documented political conduct, reversals, and unresolved claims.

An entry's inclusion does not mean every listed action was criminal or that every controversy was ultimately decided against the Republican actor involved. Some entries document individual findings within larger events. Reversals and later legal developments are retained. The objective is simple: show the record, show the source, show what happened next.

Methodology

Indexed findings are not the same thing as independent scandals.

Large events can generate multiple separately documented findings. A redistricting case, for example, may contain distinct findings for several districts. THE RECORD therefore tracks both indexed entries and the larger episodes that connect them. The database prioritizes primary evidence and preserves legal developments that complicate or reverse earlier findings.

Source priorityCourt opinions, government records, election-board findings, DOJ/FEC enforcement, congressional records, consent decrees, legislative records, then high-quality secondary reporting.
ReversalsLater appellate rulings, repeals, mootness, remedies and mixed outcomes stay attached to the entry rather than being hidden.
ChargesA charge or allegation is never displayed as a conviction. Grade E exists specifically to keep unresolved matters separate.
DuplicationRelated findings share an episode ID so the archive can count both granular findings and broader episodes without pretending they are equivalent.
CorrectionsBroken sources, missing reversals, duplicate entries, outdated legal status or misleading wording should be reported and reviewed.
